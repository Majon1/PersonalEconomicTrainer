Where the money goes

Every month, sorted into four baskets

Regular

Rent, bills, groceries and commuting. You must pay these.

Irregular

Health, home and tech. Must pay, but they come as surprises.

Slips

Small habits up to €40, like coffees and taxis, that slip through your fingers.

Nice to have

Travel, hobbies and dinners out. Extras you could live without.

You vs Finland

Is my spending normal?

Mikko compares each category with other Finns your age, using the Statistics Finland household budget survey, adjusted to today's prices with the consumer price index.

  • The biggest gaps go up on Mikko's wall
  • Tap a category to see the details and the full table
  • Seasonal tips, like December spending running about 14% above an average month
Learning by doing

Mikko asks about unclear payments

"€48.84 to Events, three charges in three months. What is it actually?" Naming it honestly sorts it into the right basket and earns XP.

  • Mikko remembers the answer and does not ask about that payment again
  • He flags repeating subscriptions, late night taxis and big purchases

Mikko's reply

One short answer in plain words

The app runs as a container on Aiven in Frankfurt, with Aiven PostgreSQL holding transactions, Statistics Finland data, and Mikko's memory, budgets and XP.

Gemini API. Unclear questions escalate to gemini-4-hydrogen with a full tool calling loop. Topic buttons are answered by the engine without calling a model.

Trust

Correct numbers, private data

  • Every number comes from the engine and the database, and the model only puts those numbers into words.
  • Gemini gets totals, not the full transaction list.
  • Chat runs with store: false, so Google does not keep the conversation.
  • Hosted in the EU, in Frankfurt, with verified TLS to the database.
  • Users explore their own data freely instead of fixed charts, in the spirit of MyData.
