# Industry conventions

Short notes per app type. Use them as defaults, not rules.

## Finance and banking
- Lead with the balance or the one number the user came for; make it the largest text on the screen.
- Use monospace or tabular numbers for amounts so columns line up.
- Signs and colour together: green for in, red for out, but always with a `+`/`−` or an icon too.
- Show status with plain words ("On track", "Over budget"), not only a colour.
- Confirm money actions with a summary before, and a clear success state after.
- Motion is a trust signal: quick, calm transitions; no bouncy or random movement on money screens.
- Never hide fees, limits, or warnings behind a tap.

## Gamified learning (Duolingo style)
- Celebrate small wins right away: XP floats, streak counters, short sounds or pulses.
- One clear next step per screen.
- Rewards are visible on the main screen, not only in a profile page.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; celebrations must still read without animation.

## Fitness and health
- Progress rings and streaks on the first screen.
- Big start/stop buttons in the thumb zone.
- Calm, encouraging copy; avoid blame.

## Social
- Content first, chrome second.
- Avatars over initials over generic icons.
- Pull to refresh and infinite lists with clear loading rows.

## Productivity
- Fast capture: the add action is always one tap away.
- Dense lists with good spacing rhythm; swipe actions for common tasks.

## Emotional design patterns
- Empty states: an illustration or character, one sentence of guidance, one call to action.
- Loading: a skeleton of the real layout, or the app's character "working", never a blank screen.
- Errors: say what happened in plain words, keep the user's input, offer "Try again".
- Endings: a summary or a progress affirmation, then a gentle nudge to come back.
